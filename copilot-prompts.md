## Tips for Best Results

1. **Regenerate multiple times** — small variations in output can make a big difference
2. **Iterate** — tell Copilot "make it simpler", "darker background", or "less detail"
3. **Be specific about what you don't want** — add "no gradients", "no people", "no 3D"
4. **Color consistency** — always specify #6366F1 (indigo purple) and #0A0A0F (dark background)
5. **Format after download** — Copilot outputs raster images (PNG). For logos, trace to SVG using Figma or Inkscape
6. **Aspect ratios** — specify the ratio in the prompt for banners and headers
