Our product
Opserax In production

Maintenance and facilities, from the request to the signed work order.

Opserax replaces spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and disconnected systems with one workspace for service requests, work orders, preventive maintenance and assets. Field teams work on mobile — even offline — while managers see everything in real time.

  • Executive dashboard with KPIs and trends
  • Preventive maintenance that schedules itself
  • Assets with QR codes, location and custom forms
  • Work orders in kanban, list, calendar and map
  • Service requests with business-hours SLA
  • Audit-ready reports in Excel and PDF
